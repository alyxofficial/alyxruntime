Who we are

Technology should be open to explore.

We bring together ideas across firmware, embedded hardware, software runtimes, and low-level computing. Alyx is a place to learn by building and to make experiments available for others to use, study, and improve.

Our focus

From firmware experiments on small devices to software that connects machines and makes their hardware useful, our work is driven by curiosity and open collaboration. Projects can take different forms, but they share one goal: give people tools they can understand, modify, and build on.
